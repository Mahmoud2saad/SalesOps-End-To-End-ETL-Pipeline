## SALES OPS DEFINITION

**Sales Operations (Sales Ops)** is a business function that supports sales teams through **data, processes, and technology** to help them sell more effectively.

### In Simple Terms:
Sales Ops is the bridge between **sales data** and **business decisions**. They're the ones who:
- Track sales performance metrics
- Build dashboards for sales leaders
- Ensure data quality in CRM systems
- Analyze what's working and what's not

---

## WHAT SALES OPS ACTUALLY DOES

| Activity | Real-World Example |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Performance Tracking** | "Which region had the highest sales this quarter?" |
| **Forecasting** | "Based on current pipeline, we'll hit $5M next month" |
| **Territory Planning** | "We need 3 more reps in the Midwest" |
| **Compensation** | "Calculate commissions for 200 sales reps" |
| **Process Optimization** | "Why are deals getting stuck in negotiation stage?" |
| **Data Hygiene** | "Fix these 500 duplicate accounts in Salesforce" |

## WHY "SALES OPS" FITS YOUR PROJECT PERFECTLY

Your data contains:
- **Orders** → Sales transactions
- **Line items** → Products sold
- **Customers** → Who bought
- **Parts/Products** → What they bought
- **Suppliers** → Who provides products

**That's EXACTLY what a Sales Ops team analyzes every day.**

Your star schema answers questions like:
- "What's our revenue by customer segment?" (dim_customer)
- "Which products are selling best?" (dim_product)
- "How do sales trend by quarter?" (dim_date)
- "What's the average order value?" (fact_orders)
